# Model card: Peer clustering

- Generated by `python scripts/run_ml.py` (seed 42); every figure below is read from that run's stored results.
- Data snapshot id: `2dca6b196267` (prices 2021-10-06 to 2026-10-06, 25 companies)

## Purpose

Check whether data-driven groups of companies match the sector labels used as peer groups in the
comparable-company analysis. Descriptive: it describes the last five years, it does not predict.

## Data

25 companies, 1,221 trading days of returns on dates common to
all of them, and each company's latest annual fundamentals from `core.metrics`.

## Features and methods

| Method | Input | Notion of similarity |
|---|---|---|
| K-means (20 initializations) | Standardized `volatility`, `beta`, `market_correlation`, `net_margin`, `roe`, `revenue_growth` | Companies with similar risk and business profile |
| Hierarchical, average linkage | Distance = 1 - correlation of daily returns | Stocks whose prices move together |

Fundamental features are limited to ones that apply to every sector type, so banks are clustered
on the same features as everyone else (EBITDA margin and debt/equity are left out). Missing
fundamentals are replaced by the cross-sectional median: 0 value(s) in this
run.
The first two principal components, used only for plotting, explain
68.7% of the feature variance.

## Choosing k

k is chosen by the highest silhouette score over k = 2..8;
K-means inertia is listed for the elbow.

| k | K-means silhouette | K-means inertia (elbow) | Hierarchical silhouette |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.258 | 104.6 | 0.155 |
| 3 | 0.309 | 71.9 | 0.245 |
| 4 | 0.306 | 57.1 | 0.261 |
| 5 | 0.309 | 45.9 | 0.241 |
| 6 | 0.287 | 38.7 | 0.268 |
| 7 | 0.291 | 32.8 | 0.236 |
| 8 | 0.267 | 29.0 | 0.234 |

Chosen: **k = 5** for K-means (silhouette 0.309)
and **k = 6** for hierarchical clustering (silhouette
0.268). Silhouette scores of this size indicate weak-to-moderate
structure, and for K-means the scores for neighbouring k are close, so its k is not sharply
determined.

## Limitations

- **25 companies, 5 per sector.** A single company changing cluster moves the ARI noticeably.
- **The universe was chosen by sector**, five well-known companies from each. Agreement between
  return-based clusters and sectors is partly a property of that selection and would be weaker in
  a broader, less tidy universe.
- **Stability is measured on resampled returns only.** Fundamentals are held fixed, and they come
  from one fiscal year.
- **K-means depends on the feature set and scaling.** A different, equally reasonable set of
  features would give different clusters; the bootstrap range above shows how loosely they hold.
- **Infosys fundamentals are in USD** (ratios are currency-invariant, growth is in USD).

## What this must not be used for

- Predicting prices or the direction of returns, or timing trades.
- Selecting, recommending or ranking securities. FinSight is an analytics tool, not investment advice.
- Any decision where the limitations above would matter and have not been checked on newer data.
